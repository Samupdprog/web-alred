# Alred Project Brief

Alred needs a bilingual website that presents the company clearly, attracts qualified leads and can later support richer analytics, backend workflows and internal dashboards.

## Business Focus

Alred offers:

- Ecommerce stores.
- Business management websites.
- Software solutions.
- Dashboards and analytics.
- SEO analytics and statistics.
- Automations.

## Product Goal

The website should feel professional, fast, structured and trustworthy. It should explain what Alred does without becoming an oversized page full of repeated information.

## Audience

Primary visitors are companies and professionals who need practical digital systems: a better website, an online store, a management tool, reporting, automation or SEO measurement.

## Editorial Tone

Use clear, confident and specific language. Avoid empty hype, excessive jargon and repeated keywords. Explain outcomes, business value and process in a way that helps a nontechnical client understand why Alred is a solid choice.
